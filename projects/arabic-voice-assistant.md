# Arabic Voice Assistant

Consulting · Insurance · Voice AI

## Problem

A Saudi insurer processes tens of thousands of prescriptions a day. Its teams had a lot of questions about that data: who did a given transaction, when it happened, how many transactions came in per day. They wanted to ask by voice, in Arabic, and hear the answer.

## What I did

- Built and deployed a voice assistant: staff ask a question out loud in Arabic, and it answers out loud from the prescriptions data.
- Adapted the speech recognition to Arabic.
- Kept the wait short by streaming the answer and splitting long answers into parts.

## Impact

- **Running in production.**
- A spoken question gets a spoken answer in **5–8 seconds**.

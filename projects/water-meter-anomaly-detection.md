# Fraud and Tampering Detection on Water Meters

Utilities · Anomaly detection

## Problem

A national water utility needed to find tampering and fraud in meter readings. A high reading can mean several very different things: a leak, a faulty meter, a property that genuinely uses a lot of water, or tampering. Only one of those is fraud, and each needs a different response.

## What I did

- Built an end-to-end pipeline that flags suspicious meters across national metering data.
- Judged each meter against its own history and against similar properties, so normal differences between properties aren't flagged.
- Kept the flags explainable, because a field team has to drive out and inspect each one.

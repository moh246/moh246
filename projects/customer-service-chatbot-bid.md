# Customer-Service Chatbot Proposal for a Bank

Banking · Solution design · Pre-sales

## Problem

A bank put out a tender for a customer-service chatbot. To win it, the proposal had to convince a non-technical evaluation committee that the solution would actually be delivered, and that it would handle the cases where the bot doesn't know the answer.

## What I did

- Designed the solution: the architecture, how the knowledge base works, and how conversations are handed over to human agents.
- Wrote the delivery plan and the proof of concept.
- Translated what the bank actually needed, beyond what the tender said, into a proposal the committee could say yes to.

## Impact

- **Won the bid.**

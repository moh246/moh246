# Voice of the Customer Reclassification

Banking · Customer experience · I led this project

## Problem

Complaints from the mobile app kept landing in an "Other" bucket. Nobody owned that bucket, so the complaints never reached the team that could fix them.

## What I did

- Led the project from business study to delivery.
- Worked with the business teams and a business translator to define what the complaints were actually about: about 12 main categories, each with about 10 subcategories.
- Built an automated classifier that sorts every complaint into its category and subcategory, so it reaches the team that owns the problem. It needed no hand-labelled training data and copes with Egyptian colloquial Arabic, which is how customers actually write.
- Validated the results with the business before rollout.
- The results fed a C-level financial impact dashboard, built by the data visualization team.

## Impact

- Classification accuracy rose from about **60%** with the old rules to **92%**.
- **50,000+ complaints** routed to the teams that could fix them.
- Quantified about **EGP 12M a year of revenue at risk**: roughly half of a customer group worth over EGP 25M in profit. The underlying issues then got fixed.

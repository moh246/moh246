# Agentic Portfolio Analytics

Banking · AI assistants

## Problem

To answer a question about the loan and card portfolio, a corporate client or a customer form, bank staff had to call colleagues or search through paper records. That took a lot of time.

## What I did

Built three AI assistants, each matched to a different kind of information:

- **Retail:** answers questions about the loan and credit-card portfolio directly from the bank's data, with charts.
- **Corporate:** answers questions about corporate clients and how the bank works with them, from internal documents.
- **Know-your-customer:** returns onboarding forms exactly as they are, or one specific piece of information from them.

Every answer is checked automatically. When an assistant can't answer reliably, it says so and suggests related questions it can answer, instead of guessing.

## Impact

| Assistant | Users |
|---|---|
| Retail | 3,000+ |
| Corporate | about 2,500 |
| Know-your-customer | 500–1,000 |

- Automated checks rated **over 90%** of answers correct.
- Staff find answers in the assistant instead of calling people or searching paper records.
